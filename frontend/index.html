        the rules of the den

        How to Play

        Shithead — sometimes called Shed — is a shedding game of bluffing and bad luck. Empty your hand before anyone else. Whoever's still holding cards when everyone else is out wears the title.

        The deal

        Every player gets nine cards: three face-down in front of you, three face-up on top of those, and a hand of three. You play from your hand first — the face-down and face-up cards stay put for now. Ranks run 2 (lowest) up to A (highest). Whoever was dealt the 4 of hearts leads the first trick.

        On your turn

        Play one card, or several of the same rank at once, equal to or higher than the top card on the pile. Can't beat it? Pick up the entire pile into your hand and play passes on.

        The special cards

        • 2 — always playable, on anything. It resets the pile, so whoever's next can follow with any card.
        • 3 — transparent. Play it like a normal card, but the next player plays against whatever card is underneath it instead.
        • 7 — flips the rule: the next card played must be a 7 or lower, until someone breaks it with a 2 or burns the pile.
        • 8 — reverses turn order. Heads-up, that just means you go again.
        • 10 — always playable (unless a 7 is in effect). Burns the pile for good, and you go again.
        • Four of a kind — four cards of the same rank stacked in a row also burns the pile, and you go again.

        Running out of hand

        Once your hand and the draw deck are both empty, play straight from your face-up cards. Once those are gone too, play blind from your face-down cards: flip one, and if it doesn't beat the pile, you pick up everything — the revealed card included.

        Winning (and losing)

        First player to clear their hand, face-up, and face-down cards is safe and out of the running. Play continues among whoever's left — the very last player still holding cards is the Shithead.
